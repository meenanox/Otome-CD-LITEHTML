Notes & proxy settings

SKiT Dolce lists are fetched with the in-stock filter turned off (so sold-out CDs are included) unless you tick the box above. Lists come through a free public CORS proxy, which can see what you look up; if it is down, edit the proxy list here. Full details for each CD are fetched one by one, so big imports take a while.

      Merge duplicates

      The same CD added more than once — matched by title (romaji or Japanese, ignoring “CV.” tags), catalog no., JAN or source page. Each group becomes one entry: the most complete one is kept, its blanks are filled from the others, and nothing is copied twice.

      Edit selected CDs

      Tick a field to change it on every selected CD. Unticked fields stay as they are; a ticked field left blank is cleared.

      Scan local folders

      Pick the folder where your drama CDs live. Only folder and file names (and sizes) are read — no files are opened, copied or uploaded, and nothing changes on your shelf until you press Apply.
